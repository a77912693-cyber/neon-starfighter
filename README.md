Pilot your interceptor through a neon asteroid belt in Neon Starfighter, a fast-paced arcade space game. Blast hostile drones, dodge hazards, collect power-ups, and chain combos up to 25x to chase your high score.
Smooth Controls: Steer using touch drag (with mobile offset), mouse, or WASD / Arrow keys with auto-firing plasma bolts
Power-Ups: Grab Rapid Fire (⚡), Aegis Shield (🛡️), and Star Magnet (🧲) drops during combat
Arcade Feel: Particle explosions, screen shake, localStorage high score saving, and synthesized Web Audio sound effects with a mute toggle
